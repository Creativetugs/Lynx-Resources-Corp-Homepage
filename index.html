OUR PRINCIPAL PROJECT

Turner’s Ridge Property

A 225-hectare early-stage exploration property in western Newfoundland and Labrador.

Turner’s Ridge is comprised of two map-staked mineral exploration licences containing nine mineral claims covering approximately 225 hectares.

Historical and recent exploration has identified lead-zinc-silver mineralization associated with brecciated dolostone, with Lynx completing soil sampling, prospecting and channel sampling in 2023.

GEOLOGICAL SETTING

A lead-zinc-silver system in western Newfoundland.

Turner’s Ridge hosts coarse to fine-grained galena with calcite, barite, pyrite and sphalerite within intensely brecciated Silurian dolostone.

The mineralization represents a Mississippi Valley-Type (MVT) lead-zinc-silver system — a stratabound, epigenetic and carbonate-hosted deposit type known worldwide for structural consistency.

Deposit Classification Summary

System Type: Mississippi Valley-Type (MVT)
Host Geology: Brecciated Silurian Dolostone
Primary Minerals: Galena, Sphalerite, Pyrite
Gangue Minerals: Calcite, Barite

Conceptual Schematic: Carbonate-Hosted MVT Structural Setting
